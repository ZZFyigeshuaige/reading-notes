来源 Pitchfork 原文
进度 正文 41 段已读完，另附导语与 31 处取材地点清单

这篇人物采访从 Ecco2k 在香港拍〈Sky Burial〉写起，追溯他如何用六年时间，把旅途的影像与实录声音做成个人专辑《Echo》。记者在听感上保留疑问，觉得极其多样的素材最终听起来相当齐整。Ecco2k 则用「崇高」串起世界令他感到的美丽与威胁。后半篇转向家人、童年好友 Bladee，以及他把一件做了六年的私人物品交给公众时的复杂心情。

导语

Drain Gang’s perfectionist speaks about the thousands of miles of brainspace and physical traveling that went into his ambitious new solo record Echo, his first in seven years.

Drain Gang 的完美主义者谈起了《Echo》背后的漫长旅程。这张野心勃勃的个人唱片是他七年来的首张，既让他跋涉千里，也让他在脑子里走了同样远的路。导语用「数千英里」同时量地理距离和心智投入。

从香港山顶开始

Paragraph 1

英文原文

The day Ecco2k shot the video for “Sky Burial,” he was running around the streets of Hong Kong searching for antennas. He eventually found two pieces from a vendor, zip-tied them together, and only then could he hike to the top of a mountain. He ascended into a cloud: no visibility, unbelievable humidity. He didn’t have as much daylight as he needed to shoot, but coming down the other side, he noticed a structure with orange lights gleaming like beast eyes in the night. In the video, he dances with a crucifix-shaped antenna, soaked in the glow of what looks like a bloodletting sun. “I do realize that it becomes biblical and symbolic, but it wasn’t my intention,” Ecco2k, whose real name is Zak Arogundade, tells me over a video call, laughing and nibbling on a wedge of cabbage. “But I don’t know. I fuck with Jesus.”

中文翻译

拍《Sky Burial》MV那天，Ecco2k满香港找天线。最后他在一个摊贩那里找到两截，用扎带捆在一起，这才徒步上山。他一头扎进云里，什么都看不见，湿气重得难以置信。白昼剩下的时间不够拍摄，但从另一侧下山时，他看见一座建筑，橙光在夜里闪着，像野兽的眼睛。视频里，他拿着十字架状的天线跳舞，浸在仿佛正在流血的太阳投下的光里。他本名扎克·阿罗贡达德，在视频通话里一边笑、一边啃着一角卷心菜，对我说：“我知道最后看起来有股《圣经》的味道，象征意味也很强，但我本来没想搞成那样。不过，谁知道呢，我还挺喜欢耶稣的。”

词汇与表达

  • zip-tie something together — 用扎带把东西捆在一起。名词 zip tie 在这里活用为动词。
  • fuck with someone or something — 喜欢或认同某人或某事。带粗口的口语，这里是对耶稣有好感，随口一句也冲淡了前文的庄严。

Paragraph 2

英文原文

Arogundade’s music squirms between cloud rap, dream pop, and experimental noise. He’s been a cornerstone of Drain Gang since its birth in the early 2010s, a fey, fastidious tinkerer who quietly helped build the Swedish collective’s concepts, clothing, videos, and general direction. But until now, he’s only released one solo album, much to the chagrin of ravenous fans who archive his social posts like they’re sacred missives and investigate every pixel of an image for possible intel. “You can observe the degradation of children over time by visiting this sub,” one person once commented in reply to an especially fried Ecco2k analysis post on Reddit.

中文翻译

阿罗贡达德的音乐在云说唱、梦幻流行和实验噪音之间扭动游走。自 Drain Gang 于 2010 年代初成立以来，他一直是这个瑞典团体的核心人物之一。他有股飘忽出尘的气质，做起东西来却格外挑剔，默默参与构筑了团体的概念、服装、影像和整体方向。可到这次之前，他只发过一张个人专辑，馋坏了那群如饥似渴的歌迷。他们像保存圣谕一样存档他的社交动态，把图片逐像素解剖，搜寻任何可能的线索。有人曾在 Reddit 上一篇脑洞尤其离谱的 Ecco2k 分析帖下留言：“只要看看这个版，就能观察到孩子们是怎么一步步退化的。”

词汇与表达

  • squirm between — 在不同风格之间扭动游走。把风格流动写成身体动作，有不安分、难归类的感觉。
  • a fey, fastidious tinkerer — 气质飘忽出尘、反复琢磨细节的手艺人。fey 在此有缥缈古怪的意味，fastidious 写他对细节的挑剔，tinkerer 写他爱动手试改。
  • much to the chagrin of — 让某人很是懊恼。把当事人的不满作为插入语，顺带调侃歌迷。
  • fried — 离谱到像是脑子烧坏了。fried 在这里调侃帖子脑洞过头。

Paragraph 3

英文原文

Echo, out today, was recorded—and filmed—across six years and a dizzying array of countries. When I speak with him, over two long calls in the weeks before the release, he’s tense yet chill, faintly ecstatic about the prospect of finally discharging it from his hard drive and out into the world. He sounds like he’s figuring out, question by question, what this album means to him and how to talk about it. “[Echo] is the most difficult thing I’ve ever done,” he says. “It’s been my whole life for the past six years. I think about it every day. It’s not an accident that my life is like this. It’s something that I’ve chosen. It was extremely gratifying, even though I didn’t enjoy every second of it.”

中文翻译

《Echo》于采访刊出当天面世，前后花了六年录音和拍摄，足迹遍布多得让人眼花的国家。在发行前几周，我和他聊了两次长长的视频通话。他紧张，又挺松弛。一想到终于能把这东西从硬盘里放出来、交到外面的世界，他隐约有些兴奋。听起来，他也在随着问题一个个抛来，慢慢弄清这张专辑对自己意味着什么，又该怎么谈它。“《Echo》是我做过最难的事。过去六年，它就是我全部的生活。我每天都在想它。过成这样是我自己选的。这件事给了我极大的满足，尽管并非每一秒都享受。”

词汇与表达

  • a dizzying array of — 种类或数量多得让人眼花缭乱。array 兼有「一大批」和「花样繁多」的意味。

Paragraph 4

英文原文

Echo’s protagonist, Echo, swims in fountains and hears birdsong in his bones; he cries about touch starvation and gets stopped at the border with flowers in his hand. He’s loveless, lawless, kissing asphalt and embracing death. Arogundade, the human wearing a shirt and grinning cheekily from a London apartment, keeps landing on the word “sublime,” specifically Edmund Burke’s philosophical definition: something “so beautiful and so grand in scale that it almost feels threatening.” The music tries to match that vertiginous feeling, with vocals like fairy hiccups and cloud rap beats that sound like falling out of the heavens. “When I first heard the ‘Sky Burial’ instrumental, I described it as a ‘Coriolis effect type beat.’ The feeling I get is like… it just invokes the whole Earth,” he marvels. “Writing happy songs or sad songs is quite straightforward. What I’m drawn to is stuff that expresses this third, almost omnipresent feeling larger than personhood or any concept of good or bad that we have.”

中文翻译

专辑里的主角也叫 Echo。他在喷泉里游泳，感觉鸟鸣在骨头里回荡。他哭诉自己太缺身体接触，又手捧鲜花在边境被拦。他无爱无律，亲吻柏油路，拥抱死亡。镜头另一端的阿罗贡达德则穿着衬衫，在伦敦的公寓里咧嘴坏笑。他反复说到「崇高」，指的是埃德蒙·伯克意义上的崇高：“美得惊人，宏大到几乎让人感到威胁。”音乐试着追上这种令人眩晕的体验，人声像仙子打嗝，云说唱的节拍像从天上栽下来。“我第一次听《Sky Burial》的伴奏，说它是‘科里奥利效应风格的 beat’。我听到的感觉就像……整个地球都被唤了出来。写快乐或悲伤的歌都比较直接。我着迷的是能表达第三种感受的东西。它仿佛无处不在，大到超越个体，也超越我们对好坏的理解。”

词汇与表达

  • touch starvation — 长期缺少身体接触而产生的强烈渴望。这里让主角的孤独具体到身体感受。
  • vertiginous — 令人有眩晕感的。把宏大带来的失重感接到人声和节拍上。
  • Coriolis effect type beat — 让人联想到地球自转的某种伴奏。制作圈常用某某名字加 type beat 指某种风格的伴奏。他把通常放艺人名的位置换成地球自转相关的物理现象。

Paragraph 5

英文原文

Arogundade points to a couplet from his song “Loveless Animal, Lawless Criminal” as the thesis of Echo: “How could I turn out so gentle when the world is so very cruel?/Still it is no less beautiful and one day it’ll kill me.” This might sound a tad formless and precious, but it’s deeply felt, and underlying everything is the life Arogundade spent feeling like an outcast: a Black kid in homogeneous Stockholm, a punk who wore makeup, the most lowkey member of a Swedish collective named Drain Gang that was mocked online (and later revered). Where someone less sure of himself might’ve ended up cold and cynical, Arogundade remains sensitive, hoping to finally be understood.

中文翻译

阿罗贡达德说，《Loveless Animal, Lawless Criminal》里相连的两句歌词就是《Echo》的主旨：“世界如此残酷，我怎么长成了这么温柔的人？／可世界依然那么美，总有一天它会杀了我。”这样的表达或许有些飘，也略带矫饰，但他的感受是真的。底下压着的是他一路走来身为局外人的经验：在族裔构成相对单一的斯德哥尔摩长大的黑人小孩、会化妆的朋克，以及曾遭网民嘲笑、后来又被捧上神坛的瑞典团体 Drain Gang 里最低调的那个人。换个对自己没那么笃定的人，或许早就变得冷淡而愤世嫉俗了。他却依然敏感，还在盼着有一天能被理解。

词汇与表达

  • formless and precious — 有些空泛又略显矫饰。precious 在审美批评里可以指刻意讲究到显得做作。记者承认概念有点飘，同时听出了真情实感。

从《E》到《PXE》与《Echo》

Paragraph 6

英文原文

Arogundade, the child of a makeup artist mom and a writer-designer dad, wasn’t primarily a musician at first. OG fans knew him as Drain Gang’s secret weapon, someone who worked behind the scenes and only secondarily put out the odd single (“All I wanna see is 1080p/But reality keep me on 240,” goes an early line). He’s still a craftsman—he’s working on a line of ceramics, and a perfume that he’s spent 10 years developing—and fans still consider him the recluse in a collective known for shunning the light, a perfectionist who didn’t release a solo album until six years into his career. That solo album, 2019’s E, is a poignant coming-of-age document about the divide between who you are inside and how you’re perceived. His teammates Bladee and Thaiboy Digital were wounded and poetic, but Arogundade’s music had a giggly and damaged immediacy all his own, like a hurt angel locking eyes with you.

中文翻译

阿罗贡达德的母亲是化妆师，父亲是写作者兼设计师。他最初并不以音乐为主。老粉把他视作 Drain Gang 的秘密武器。他主要在幕后干活，写歌只是顺带，偶尔发首单曲。早期有句歌词唱的是：“我只想看见 1080p，可现实偏只给我 240p。”他仍然像个手艺人，最近在做一个陶瓷系列，还有一款已经开发十年的香水。在这个出了名不爱抛头露面的团体里，歌迷还是认为他最深居简出。他是个完美主义者，入行六年才发首张个人专辑。那张于 2019 年面世的《E》动人地记录了一个人如何长大，写的是内在的自己与别人眼中的自己之间的裂缝。队友 Bladee 和 Thaiboy Digital 的音乐带着伤痛和诗意，而他有一种独有的、咯咯笑着又带伤的直抵人心的力量，像个受伤的天使与你对视。

词汇与表达

  • put out the odd single — 偶尔发一首单曲。the odd 加单数名词表示偶尔来一个。
  • shun the light — 刻意避开公众视线。这里的光指聚光灯和曝光。
  • giggly and damaged immediacy — 带着笑声和伤痕、一下就能触到人的感觉。三个词叠起来写听感。immediacy 指情绪仿佛直接抵达听者。

Paragraph 7

英文原文

PXE, the 10-minute EP that followed, was prettier still, with lyrics that framed a dialogue between “Ecco” (timid, sunken in his skin) and “Echo” (confident, with a blonde wig and makeup trailing down his face). Ecco was meant to be the protagonist of E, while Echo was a prophecy. “A big part of the EP was about this feeling of wanting to become this future version of yourself, but you just don’t know how to get there yet,” he tells me.

中文翻译

随后的十分钟 EP《PXE》听来更漂亮。歌词搭起了两个人物的对话：「Ecco」胆怯，仿佛整个人往自己的皮肤里缩；「Echo」很自信，戴着金色假发，妆顺着脸淌下来。Ecco 原本是《E》的主角，Echo 则预示着未来。“这张 EP 有很大一部分在讲一种感受：你想变成未来的那个自己，可你还不知道该怎么走到那里。”

Paragraph 8

英文原文

So when PXE came out, he’d basically already conceived of Echo—he just needed to figure out how to get there. Continuing an approach he tried on E’s “Fruit Bleed Juice,” Arogundade built the album like a composer scoring a film, producing music from footage collected on his travels. Between trips, he’d sit down with his laptop and process all the recordings and impressions, pairing them with vague song ideas in his head. He’d then flesh out the structures and write the songs while watching the footage on a screen. Then he’d go on more trips, recording and writing more. It wasn’t until four years into his nomadic spree that he started to narrow down his ideas and roughly sequence things. Some traveling was done alone, some with friends, and some with his mom, who helped capture footage. Arogundade’s painstaking approach involved visiting the same far-flung location multiple times to re-shoot footage if that base footage was what inspired the audio. The work feels almost excruciatingly sweated over, to the point where I wonder to myself if trying to serve up the sublime as a buffet negates the idea of the sublime at all.

中文翻译

《PXE》问世时，他其实已经构想出了《Echo》，只是还得弄明白怎么抵达它。他延续自己在《E》的〈Fruit Bleed Juice〉里试过的做法，像给电影配乐一样做专辑，从旅途中收集的影像里生出音乐。每次旅行之间，他坐到笔记本电脑前梳理录音和见闻，把它们同脑海里模糊的歌曲构想配对。接着他一边看屏幕里的素材，一边把结构做扎实，把歌写出来。然后继续上路，再录，再写。这样四处跑了四年，他才开始收拢想法，给作品大致排出顺序。旅程有时独自走，有时与朋友同行，也有时带母亲一起去，由她帮忙拍摄。要是某段原始画面启发了音乐，他甚至肯一次次回到同一个遥远的地点补拍。作品处处能听出下了多少苦功，几乎有点让人替他受累。我甚至忍不住想，要是把崇高铺陈成任人取用的自助餐，是否就把崇高本身给抵消了？

词汇与表达

  • score a film — 为一部电影配乐。这里先有画面，再由画面催生音乐。
  • flesh out the structures — 把原本只有轮廓的结构充实起来。flesh out 常用于把构想、故事或计划从骨架做成完整形态。
  • sweat over something — 为某件事费尽心力、反复打磨。excruciatingly 加重了记者的保留，精工细作到了让人替他觉得累的地步。

Paragraph 9

英文原文

A seven-year gap between solo albums would’ve been a long time even pre-streaming. Arogundade is 32 now. How funny that Stockholm produced both Spotify, the engine that devalued music and incentivizes constant data dumps, and the data dump’s biggest opp. “I can’t really imagine sitting in my fucking rocking chair when I’m old, being like, ’Damn, I wish I would’ve put out more junk,’” he says.

中文翻译

两张个人专辑隔七年，即使在流媒体出现以前也够久了。阿罗贡达德现在 32 岁。有意思的是，斯德哥尔摩既出了 Spotify 这台让音乐贬值、鼓励人不断往上倒内容的机器，也出了最不肯配合这种内容倾倒的人。“我可想象不出自己老了坐在他妈的摇椅上说，‘操，当年要是多发点垃圾就好了。’”

词汇与表达

  • someone’s biggest opp — 某人或某种做法最大的对头。opp 是 opposition 或 opponent 的俚语缩写。记者把大批量上传内容写成有敌人的一方。

Paragraph 10

英文原文

“A really nice part of being able to take longer is that in a year, maybe you get to experience one or two things that are really special,” Arogundade continues. But with so many years at his disposal to find serendipitous moments, he wasn’t forced to incorporate anything that he didn’t think was ultra-magical; he could make a dense collage out of what he believed were perfect clips. Take his video for “Aldehydes and Ketones,” a silver-skied UK bass song that seems to narrate scenes of ecological devastation. 30 hours of footage taken across five years were compressed into a four-minute highlight reel of skylines and mystical lights.

中文翻译

“能花更长时间的一个好处是，一年里也许能遇到一两件真正特别的事。”阿罗贡达德接着说。手头有这么多年的时间可等偶然降临的好东西，他便不用勉强收进任何自己觉得还没那么神奇的素材，能把心目中最完美的片段拼成一幅密密匝匝的拼贴。比如〈Aldehydes and Ketones〉的 MV。那是首仿佛笼在银色天空下的英式贝斯音乐，像在讲生态遭到摧毁的场景。五年拍下的三十小时素材被压进四分钟，剪成一辑天际线与神秘光影的集锦。

词汇与表达

  • at someone’s disposal — 可由某人随意支配。常修饰时间、资金或工具。此处的资源正是多年创作时间。
  • serendipitous moments — 无意间撞见的好时刻。serendipitous 既强调偶然，也强调偶然发现的东西值得珍惜。

声音如何做出来

Paragraph 12

英文原文

It’s strange, because this detail should make the album completely unlike anything he’s done before, sucking the globe into Arogundade’s peculiar orbit. But Echo often feels like a grander version of E’s delicate cloud&B; it gestures toward experimentation, with flashes of electricity and stripped-back ballads, but everything has been softened into a kind of mature uniformity.

中文翻译

奇怪的是，按理说，这份行程应该让这张唱片跟他以前的作品完全不同，仿佛把整个地球都吸进了阿罗贡达德自己的古怪轨道。可《Echo》常听起来像《E》那种轻盈云雾般的节奏布鲁斯的放大版。它有走向实验的姿态，有电光一闪的爆发，也有编配精简的抒情曲，但整体都磨圆了，形成一种成熟而齐整的音色。

词汇与表达

  • gesture toward experimentation — 显露出尝试实验的倾向。gesture toward 带一点保留，表示做出姿态或给出迹象，力度弱于彻底转向。
  • stripped-back ballads — 编配精简的抒情曲。stripped-back 描述伴奏铺陈少，核心旋律或人声因此更突出。
