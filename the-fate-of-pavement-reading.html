Pavement 的命运

作者 Grayson Haver Currin · 摄影 Brian Karlsson · 2026 年 11 月 10 日
来源 Pitchfork 原文
进度 正文 66 段全部完成，另附导语

亚特兰大一场因雷暴取消的演出，把 Pavement 的过去和现在摆到了一起。记者从后台回溯他们的自制黑胶、九十年代差一点到手的巨星生涯、两次重聚，以及一首冷门歌在网上爆红后带来的新听众。贯穿全文的是谁能决定接下来发生什么。成员能把旧歌练得更稳，也更懂得怎样相处，但没有人能控制一场暴雨、一次走红，或者这段友谊还可以带他们走多远。

导语

For more than three decades, Pavement have been synonymous with indie rock. Five years into their improbable second reunion, can they be more than what they have already been?

三十多年来，Pavement 几乎就是独立摇滚的代名词。谁也没料到的第二次重聚已经走到第五年，他们还可能成为某种超出既有身份的乐队吗？

一场未能开演的压轴

Paragraph 1

英文原文

Pavement should be on in 37 minutes. On a steamy Saturday night in Atlanta, they should headline the Piedmont stage, one of three enormous platforms at the Shaky Knees festival, tucked into the corner of a 200-acre park two miles north of downtown. And the band should play at the same time as the Prodigy, English electronic miscreants that two members confess might have frightened them a tad in the ’90s, or at least made them feel like the nice young indie-rock boys.

中文翻译

再过 37 分钟，Pavement 就该登台了。亚特兰大一个闷热的周六夜，按安排，他们将在 Shaky Knees 音乐节的皮德蒙特舞台压轴。音乐节三座巨型舞台之一，就搭在市中心以北两英里处一座两百英亩公园的角落。照理说，他们会和英国电子乐的那帮坏小子 The Prodigy 同时开演。有两位成员承认，九十年代的他们没准还真有点怕 The Prodigy，至少在对方跟前觉得自己活像一群玩独立摇滚的乖孩子。

词汇与表达

  • should be on — 按安排就该登台演出。连用几个 should，让一切听来已有定数，同时给后面的取消埋下伏笔。

Paragraph 2

英文原文

Getting here for this one-off gig—the last show of their improbable second reunion, which began in 2022—has been a production: Guitarist Spiral Stairs came in from Australia, where he’s lived for four years, while singer Stephen Malkmus came from Chicago, days after dropping off his youngest daughter, Sunday, at a Philadelphia college. Bob Nastanovich, Pavement’s auxiliary drummer and tireless hypeman onstage and off, drove the hour north from the spot he recently bought in Macon, having shaped a 15-song setlist that scans like a Pavement primer. They are unrehearsed, but Nastanovich exudes the optimism of a man addicted to horse betting for 45 years, just as he has been. “It’s all songs we’ve played a number of times, songs we have a lot of control over,” he tells me of his setlist, his Southern accent so warm and welcoming I’d buy almost anything he was selling.

中文翻译

为了赶来演这一场，大家费了不少周折。这原本是乐队自 2022 年开始的、那次谁也没想到的二度重聚的收官演出。吉他手 Spiral Stairs 从住了四年的澳大利亚飞来，主唱 Stephen Malkmus 则从芝加哥赶来，几天前才送小女儿 Sunday 去费城上大学。Bob Nastanovich 是乐队的辅助鼓手，在台上台下都不知疲倦地给大家带气氛。他从最近在梅肯买下的住处往北开了一小时，排了十五首歌，光看曲目就像一份 Pavement 入门指南。他们还没排练，但 Nastanovich 浑身散发着一个赌了四十五年赛马的人才有的乐观。他也确实赌了这么久。“都是演过好多回的歌，我们拿捏得住。”他跟我说。他的南方口音暖得亲切，几乎他说什么我都会信。

词汇与表达

  • be a production — 办起来费了好大一番周折。口语里形容一件事搞得阵仗大。
  • scan like a primer — 读起来像是一份入门指南。scan like 在这里说扫一眼给人的观感，primer 是初学者读物。

Paragraph 3

英文原文

But what control? At 7:53 p.m., bassist Mark Ibold emerges with a black garbage bag and kneels in front of a refrigerator in one of two backstage trailers. He shovels rows of Modelo and Coors Light inside. The back of his shirt reads “Poachers Bar” in big blue letters, a name almost too perfect for what happens in almost every rock band’s dressing room when the show gets canceled, which just happened to this one—you grab the beer and go.

中文翻译

可他们又能拿捏什么？晚上 7 点 53 分，贝斯手 Mark Ibold 拿了个黑色垃圾袋出来，在后台两辆拖车之一的冰箱前跪下，一排排地往袋里塞 Modelo 和 Coors Light 啤酒。他的衬衫背后蓝色大字印着「Poachers Bar」。几乎每个摇滚乐队取消演出后，化妆间里都会发生同一件事，而他们刚刚就碰上了。这名字实在太应景了：抄上啤酒就走。

背景补充

Poachers 是偷猎者，也让人想到顺手把本该留在后台的东西带走。

Paragraph 4

英文原文

Thunder shakes the trailer and lightning speckles the sky. Soaked fans scurry toward gates. I find Nastanovich next door, unperturbed and streaming a horse race from Altoona, Iowa, on his laptop. Brinx Job—the second horse he named for a Pavement song, after Range Life—is about to run. When I tell Nastanovich the news, he shrugs, clutches his Coors, and points at his laptop. “The show’s right here, my man.” I put $20 on Brinxy, as Nastanovich calls him, who barely holds on to third. We all lose.

中文翻译

雷声震得拖车直晃，闪电在天上四处亮起。浑身湿透的观众纷纷奔向出口。我在隔壁找到 Nastanovich。他丝毫没乱阵脚，正用笔记本电脑看艾奥瓦州阿尔图纳的赛马直播。Brinx Job 就要开跑了，这是他继 Range Life 之后，第二匹用 Pavement 歌名命名的马。听我说演出取消，他耸耸肩，攥着 Coors，朝笔记本一指：“哥们儿，好戏在这儿呢。”我给他口中的「小 Brinx」下了二十美元。这马勉强保住第三。我们全输了。

词汇与表达

  • barely hold on to third — 勉强保住第三名。hold on to 指已经在这个位置，险些被反超。

Paragraph 5

英文原文

A few minutes later, the band and crew squeeze into a white van and head for the hotel. Over his shoulder, drummer Steve West tells me about the only other canceled Pavement show he remembers, at a Spanish festival in 1997 when a storm blew in so fast the stage almost collapsed on the crew. He’s bummed, but this wasn’t that bad. Up front, after all, Ibold is clutching his bag of beers; the band will walk tonight with their full fee, a few hundred grand according to industry sources, a lot more than they ever made back then.

中文翻译

几分钟后，乐队和工作人员挤进一辆白色面包车，往酒店去。鼓手 Steve West 回过头跟我说，他只记得还有一次 Pavement 演出被取消，那是 1997 年在西班牙的一场音乐节。暴风雨来得太快，舞台差点倒下来砸到工作人员。他挺沮丧，但这次还没那么糟。毕竟前排的 Ibold 正紧紧抱着那袋啤酒，而且乐队今晚能拿足演出费。业内人士说有几十万美元，比他们当年拿到的多得多。

词汇与表达

  • walk with the full fee — 走的时候把全额报酬拿到手。walk with 这里强调最终能带走什么。

Paragraph 6

英文原文

The hotel bar is already a crowded Pavement hang when we arrive. A crew of Nastanovich’s Macon friends made the trek, while a North Carolina contingent includes the cofounder of the company that had long printed Pavement’s merch. Stairs never made it to the park; he spots me, crosses the room, and grins. “Stephen said this afternoon, ‘I hope this gets canceled.’ You saw him at sound check—he wasn’t feeling it,” he says. “I told him, ‘Well, you got your wish.’ ” This is the last announced Pavement show to date, though several are scheduled for 2027; crew and band clump together, catching up before saying goodbye. “See you next year,” I hear someone say.

中文翻译

我们到的时候，酒店酒吧已经挤满与 Pavement 有关的人。Nastanovich 在梅肯的一群朋友专程赶来，北卡罗来纳来的一拨人里，还有一家长期给乐队印周边的公司的联合创办人。Stairs 压根没去成公园。他认出我，穿过酒吧，咧嘴笑道：“Stephen 下午说，‘希望它取消。’试音你也见到了，他没那个兴致。我跟他说，‘行啊，你如愿了。’”这是迄今官宣的最后一场，尽管 2027 年还有几场已排上。乐队和工作人员聚成一团，在告别前叙叙旧。我听见有人说：“明年见。”

词汇与表达

  • make the trek — 专程跋涉赶来。trek 在口语里强调路途费时费力。
  • not be feeling it — 当时提不起兴致或没进入状态。这里谈的是试音和当天的心情。

Paragraph 7

英文原文

Pavement as a band was unlikely from the beginning: Two elementary-school friends, Malkmus and Stairs, started the band as an art project while back home from college in Northern California. They unwittingly enlisted Gary Young, a drummer 13 years their senior who had recorded them in his home studio, dubbed Louder Than You Think. An inveterate showman, he would greet every concertgoer at the door or hand them celery sticks or mashed potatoes or even pennies as they entered; he partied so hard on tour he once fell backward from his drums through a curtain behind an eight-foot stage in Atlanta, though he crawled back up to the kit and finished the gig anyway. They recruited Nastanovich as a supporting drummer—his only real qualification being that he could keep time—and Ibold, who first picked up a bass at 26 and nearly 40 years later hesitates to call himself a bassist. “It was kind of like, ‘Oh, that sounds fun,’ but I never felt like, ‘Oh, this is a band,’ ” Stairs says of Pavement’s existence.

中文翻译

Pavement 从一开始就不像会成为一支乐队。Malkmus 和 Stairs 是小学朋友，读大学时回到北加州老家，把组乐队当成一个艺术项目。他们稀里糊涂地拉来大自己十三岁的鼓手 Gary Young。他之前在自家的录音室 Louder Than You Think 给他俩录过音。Young 天生爱表演，会在门口迎接每位观众，有时还给进门的人发芹菜条、土豆泥，甚至几枚硬币。有一回在亚特兰大，他巡演途中玩得太凶，从鼓凳上仰面倒下，穿过帘子摔下八英尺高的舞台，却又爬回鼓组把演出打完。他们招来 Nastanovich 当辅助鼓手，他唯一拿得出手的资格就是能稳住拍子。还有 Ibold，他二十六岁才第一次拿起贝斯，将近四十年过去，还是不好意思自称贝斯手。说到 Pavement 的存在，Stairs 说：“大概就是，‘哦，听着挺好玩。’我从没想过，‘哦，这是一支乐队。’”

词汇与表达

  • an inveterate showman — 走到哪儿都爱把场面变成表演的人。inveterate 强调习性根深蒂固。
  • keep time — 稳稳地维持节拍。这里是鼓手最基本的工作。

Paragraph 8

英文原文

But still, Pavement’s five-album run between 1992 and 1999 yielded two, maybe three masterpieces and a catalog so rich the band can now call up nearly 100 tunes. Pavement is perhaps the most efficient metonym for indie rock too. The band’s inscrutable lyrics and ramshackle approach make for songs that feel like unintentional and effortless anthems, rock ’n’ roll castoffs that catalyzed new possibilities. “A lot of the charm of Pavement originally was that I was doing it wrong,” admits Malkmus, noting the way he sang beyond his range with a high giggle. “It’s out of tune. It signified youth and enthusiasm, lo-fi.”

中文翻译

即便如此，Pavement 从 1992 年到 1999 年连发五张专辑，出了两张、也许三张杰作。曲库丰厚到今天乐队还能随时调出近百首歌。也许只要说出 Pavement 这个名字，就最能让人明白什么是独立摇滚。难解的歌词、摇摇晃晃的演奏做法，竟弄出一首首仿佛无心插柳、毫不费力就能让人合唱的歌。这些像被摇滚乐扔掉的边角料，反倒催生了新的可能。“Pavement 最初很大一部分魅力，是我唱得不对。”Malkmus 承认，说起他怎样带着高高的咯咯笑声唱过自己够得到的音域。“是走音的。那传出来的是年轻、热情，还有低保真。”

词汇与表达

  • a metonym for indie rock — 一个名字就能代指出独立摇滚。metonym 是转喻，用与某概念紧密相连的东西来指代它。
  • ramshackle approach — 松散、摇摇晃晃的做法。ramshackle 原可形容快散架的建筑。这里形容表演和制作保留歪斜松动的质感。

Paragraph 9

英文原文

Where Pavement’s start was unlikely, though, their endurance has been uncanny, something they neither could have predicted nor can now entirely control after a reunion in 2010 and another in 2022 that continues still. In the quarter-century since they first quit, their appeal has become wildly trans-generational: They are Gen X icons whose “5-4=Unity” is used as NPR bumper music at least a dozen times each day and whose former EP obscurity “Harness Your Hopes” was sucked into the streaming algorithm and has gone viral at least twice during the past decade. It most famously soundtracked the Utah fit-check trend, where a shaggy-haired Utah teen with a cross in his TikTok handle stood in front of his pickup wearing a camo shirt. He tugged his baggy jeans, leapt into the air, and just barely landed his 720, all as “Harness Your Hopes” played.

中文翻译

如果说 Pavement 的开端出人意料，他们能撑这么久就更近乎不可思议了。2010 年重聚一次，2022 年又来一次，至今仍在继续。这种生命力他们事前想不到，现在也没法完全左右。第一次散伙后的二十五年里，他们的吸引力跨过了好几代人。他们是 X 世代的偶像，〈5-4=Unity〉每天至少十几次被美国全国公共广播电台用作转场音乐。一度藏在 EP 里的冷门歌〈Harness Your Hopes〉也被卷进流媒体算法，过去十年里至少爆红过两次。最出名的是给犹他穿搭展示风潮配乐。一个头发蓬乱、TikTok 名字里带十字架符号的犹他少年，穿迷彩衫站在皮卡前，拽了拽宽大的牛仔裤，腾空转了两整圈，险险落地，背景一直放着那首歌。

词汇与表达

  • bumper music — 广播节目用于进出一段内容的转场音乐。bumper 指节目段落之间的短过门。
  • fit-check trend — 在短视频里展示当日穿搭的风潮。fit 是 outfit 的口语简写。

Paragraph 10

英文原文

It was inane internet bullshit, but Pavement leaned into it. Everyone except Nastanovich made adorable old-guy clips of their own, Malkmus tugging on his white oxford-cloth button-down and laughing at: his dance moves, himself, his consternation with the whole ordeal? They released a music video starring Sophie Thatcher, born a year after “Harness Your Hopes” was released; in the video, she tried to decode what actually might rhyme with Pavement and imagined herself leading an indie-rock band of her own, a phenomenon Pavement had already inspired for decades.

中文翻译

这就是互联网上无聊的破玩意儿，可 Pavement 还顺势玩了起来。除了 Nastanovich，大家各拍了一段可爱的老大叔版视频。Malkmus 拽着自己的白色牛津纺领尖扣衬衫，笑他的舞步、笑他自己，还是笑他面对整件事那副不知所措的样子？他们发了支 MV，请来 Sophie Thatcher 主演，她出生时这首歌已经发行一年了。片子里，她试着破解究竟什么词能跟 Pavement 押韵，还想象自己成了一支独立摇滚乐队的主唱。几十年来，Pavement 早已让不少人真的这么做了。

词汇与表达

  • lean into it — 顺势接受并主动投入其中。常指把本可回避的事接过来做文章。

Paragraph 11

英文原文

And for Pavements, Alex Ross Perry’s 2024 movie about the band, the director staged an Off Broadway musical of its songs, built a half-fake memorabilia museum, and assembled a comedic cast that included Joe Keery and Tim Heidecker just to lampoon how seriously classic-rock bio-pics take themselves and their subjects. Sometimes frustrating and sometimes hilarious, it at least upended the form, just as Pavement once frustrated ideas of rock itself.

中文翻译

至于 Alex Ross Perry 在 2024 年拍的乐队电影《Pavements》，导演用他们的歌排了部外百老汇音乐剧，搭了间半真半假的纪念品展馆，又凑起一批喜剧面孔，其中有 Joe Keery 和 Tim Heidecker，就为了揶揄经典摇滚传记片那副把自己和主人公都太当回事的派头。电影有时让人烦，有时又实在好笑。它至少把传记片的形式翻了个底朝天，就像 Pavement 当初也让人没法照旧理解摇滚。

词汇与表达

  • lampoon something — 用夸张戏仿公开揶揄某事。lampoon 常指以讽刺作品嘲弄对象。

Paragraph 12

英文原文

On the Sunday morning after the canceled gig, Nastanovich woke up early and loaded his RAV4, driving much of the band’s gear seven hours north to West’s home in Virginia amid the Blue Ridge Mountains. The rest of the band flew up and rendezvoused to rehearse for a recording session, several people not in but around Pavement told me. (“No comment,” anyone I asked in the band’s organization said, often with a smile.) As Pavement reach the end of the fifth year of their second reunion, the indie-rock exemplars are staring down a series of existential questions: How long can you play the past before it becomes only a cash-in? How do you augment a legacy held in such utter esteem? And how does a band that helped frame rock’s future now shape its own, especially since all of this late success remains so unexpected and unpredictable?

中文翻译

演出取消后的周日一早，Nastanovich 起床把器材塞进自己的 RAV4，往北开了七小时，把乐队相当一部分装备送到蓝岭山脉间、West 在弗吉尼亚的家。几位与乐队来往密切的圈外人告诉我，其他成员飞去会合，为进棚录音排练。（问到乐队团队的人，得到的都是“不予置评”，往往还带着笑。）第二次重聚来到第五年末，这支独立摇滚的代表乐队面前摆着几道关乎存续的问题：旧歌能演多久，才会沦为纯粹捞钱？已经备受推崇的声名上，还能添些什么？一支参与塑造摇滚未来的乐队，要怎样塑造自己的未来，尤其当这一轮迟来的成功本就出乎意料，下一步也无从预测？

词汇与表达

  • stare down existential questions — 直面一连串关乎自身存续的问题。stare down 带着迎面扛住压力的感觉。
  • a cash-in — 趁热度或名气捞一笔钱的行动。这里质疑重聚演出何时会变成单纯变现。

Paragraph 13

英文原文

When I talk to Malkmus several days after the Atlanta show, he doesn’t mention these new songs. But he does tell me multiple times how much the cancellation disappointed him, no matter how he felt that afternoon. He recalls a nice couple he met in the airport who had flown across the country to see them. “I’m feeling bad for all those people who missed it and had to go see Twenty One Pilot,” he says, not quite landing the name of the night’s headliners, who got to play after a weather delay. He laughs in a way that reminds me of the 27-year-old smart aleck who, in 1994, used the last verse of one of Pavement’s most magnetic songs, “Range Life,” to rib the Smashing Pumpkins’ weird ascent and the Stone Temple Pilots’ devilish beauty and rock stardom writ large, at the very moment they seemed on the precipice of stardom themselves, before it all slowly unraveled.

中文翻译

亚特兰大演出过了几天，我和 Malkmus 聊起来。他没提这些新歌，但好几次说，不论那天下午他是什么心情，取消演出都让他很失望。他想起在机场碰到一对人很好的伴侣，专程横穿美国飞来看他们。“我替错过演出、只好去看 Twenty One Pilot 的人难过。”他把当晚另一组压轴的名字说漏了个音，天气延误过后，那组上台了。他笑起来，我又想到了 1994 年那个二十七岁、爱逞口舌之快的人。那时他在 Pavement 最抓人的歌之一〈Range Life〉的最后一段拿 The Smashing Pumpkins 的蹿红、Stone Temple Pilots 那股邪门的美感，以及摇滚明星这回事开涮。偏偏当时他们自己好像也就要成名了，然后一切慢慢散了架。

词汇与表达

  • something writ large — 某种现象的整体或放大体现。这里从两组具体乐队扩大到整个摇滚明星文化。
  • on the precipice of stardom — 站在即将成名的边缘。precipice 字面是悬崖，给走红也添上一点风险感。

英文原文
